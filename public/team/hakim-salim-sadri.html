Hakim Salim Sadri

Doctor

Born in 1910, Hakim Salim Sadri was another exceptional doctor who graduated from Lucknow. He established his practice in Sitamarhi. Following the communal tensions of 1964, a village named Garda in Simdega was left devastated with only 17 survivors. He was entrusted with the massive responsibility of rebuilding the village. Through pure dedication and hard work, he successfully helped the survivors resettle and rebuild their lives by 1965.

Hakim Salim Sadri is remembered as one of the most distinguished and respected members of the Sadri family. His life reflects educational excellence, humanitarian leadership, and unwavering commitment to serving society. Known for his intelligence, compassion, and extraordinary sense of responsibility, he earned lasting admiration as both a renowned physician and an inspiring community builder.

After completing his prestigious medical education in Lucknow, Hakim Salim Sadri began his highly successful medical practice in Sitamarhi. His expertise in diagnosis and treatment, combined with his gentle nature and ethical approach, made him one of the most trusted doctors in the region. Patients from near and far visited him for his effective care, professional excellence, and compassionate service.

His clinic became a center of healing, hope, and confidence. He was widely appreciated for his dedication to helping people and for his commitment to improving the well-being of the community. His reputation as a skilled doctor and noble human being spread throughout Bihar and neighboring regions.

Hakim Salim Sadri’s greatest achievement came when he was entrusted with the reconstruction of Garda. Through remarkable leadership, strategic planning, and relentless effort, he helped restore homes, rebuild livelihoods, and revive the spirit of the survivors. His successful rehabilitation work stands as a powerful example of humanitarian service and community development.

Today, Hakim Salim Sadri is honored as a legendary doctor, visionary leader, and humanitarian icon. His inspiring biography continues to symbolize compassion, integrity, education, and service. His enduring legacy remains a source of immense pride for the Sadri family and an inspiration for future generations.
