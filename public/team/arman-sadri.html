Arman Sadri

Leader (Zila Upadhyaksh)

Currently serving as the Rashtriya Janata Dal (RJD) District Vice President (Zila Upadhyaksh) of Samastipur, is a highly respected and influential political leader whose name commands recognition and admiration across the region. Widely known for his strong public presence and his “Robinhood” image, Arman Sadri has earned a reputation as a fearless social worker and people-centric politician who consistently stands with the poor, marginalized, and underprivileged sections of society. His political influence extends deep within Rashtriya Janata Dal, with close ties to legendary leader Lalu Prasad Yadav and the prominent Yadav family, making him one of the most trusted and powerful grassroots leaders in Bihar politics.

Even today, hundreds of people from Tajpur, Samastipur, and neighboring districts gather daily at the Arman Sadri factory in Tajpur to present their personal, social, and administrative concerns. Whether the issue involves land disputes, employment assistance, education support, healthcare needs, or government-related matters, Arman Sadri listens to every individual with patience, compassion, and dedication, and works tirelessly to ensure practical and timely solutions. This unwavering commitment to public service has made him a symbol of hope and justice for countless families throughout Bihar.

In 2005, recognizing his extraordinary popularity, organizational strength, and deep connection with the people, Lalu Prasad Yadav personally called Arman Sadri and offered him the opportunity to contest as an MLA candidate from Samastipur. Although the ticket was ultimately not granted due to internal political lobbying and strategic considerations, the offer itself reflected the immense trust and confidence placed in his leadership abilities by the highest ranks of the party. Despite this setback, Arman Sadri remained steadfast in his mission to serve the public and continued to devote his energy to social welfare and political activism.

Today, Arman Sadri is widely regarded as one of the most dynamic and influential RJD leaders in Bihar. His leadership is defined by integrity, accessibility, and an unwavering dedication to the welfare of the people. Through his tireless efforts, strong political network, and lifelong commitment to justice and development, Arman Sadri continues to shape the future of Samastipur and inspire a new generation of leaders. For many, he is not just a politician but a true जनसेवक (public servant), a champion of the common people, and a trusted voice for progress, empowerment, and social change in Bihar.
