Dr. Mohammad Makhmoor Sadri

Prominent Urdu Scholar | Researcher | Literary Critic | Academician

Dr. Mohammad Makhmoor Sadri is a distinguished Indian Urdu scholar, academician, researcher, and literary critic known for his remarkable contribution to Urdu literature, progressive criticism, and the historical study of Urdu journalism. Through his scholarly writings, academic engagements, and literary research, he has established himself as an influential voice in contemporary Urdu intellectual discourse.

With decades of dedication to Urdu studies, Dr. Sadri’s work reflects a deep commitment to preserving the literary, cultural, and journalistic heritage of the Urdu language while critically examining its social, political, and progressive dimensions.

Academic Background

Dr. Mohammad Makhmoor Sadri possesses an outstanding academic record, having achieved First Division honors throughout his educational journey. He completed his higher studies in Urdu literature with distinction and was awarded a Ph.D. in 2006. He also successfully qualified the prestigious National Eligibility Test (NET), further strengthening his academic credentials.

His academic excellence and scholarly capabilities led to his association with several renowned educational institutions and universities in India.

Research Specializations

Dr. Mohammad Makhmoor Sadri’s research primarily focuses on:

  • Progressive Urdu Literary Criticism
  • History of Urdu Journalism
  • Urdu Fiction and Narrative Techniques
  • Socio-Political Dimensions of Urdu Literature
  • Anti-Colonial Urdu Press During the 1857 Uprising
  • Evolution of Modern Urdu Prose and Literary Forms

His scholarly approach combines historical investigation with critical literary analysis, making his research highly valuable for students, researchers, and scholars of Urdu literature.

Contribution to Urdu Journalism Research

One of Dr. Sadri’s most notable scholarly areas is the study of 19th-century Urdu journalism and its relationship with political consciousness and resistance movements in colonial India.

His research explores:

  • The role of Urdu newspapers in shaping public opinion
  • Anti-colonial narratives in early Urdu journalism
  • Literary and political activism through print media
  • The influence of regional Urdu publications in Delhi and Lucknow

He has extensively analyzed historically significant newspapers such as:

  • Sadiq-ul-Akhbar (Delhi)
  • Sahar-e-Samiri (Lucknow)

Through these studies, Dr. Sadri has contributed significantly to understanding the role of Urdu journalism in India’s intellectual and nationalist history.

Major Published Works

Urdu Main Taraqqi Pasand Tanqeed

One of Dr. Sadri’s most acclaimed scholarly works, this book critically examines the evolution of Progressive literary criticism in Urdu literature. It discusses Marxist thought, scientific criticism, realism, and the ideological foundations of progressive literary movements in Urdu poetry and prose.

Taraqqi Pasand Afsane Mein Haiyet Aur Taknik Ke Tajareb

A significant academic study focusing on structural experimentation and narrative techniques within Progressive Urdu short stories. The work analyzes artistic form, literary innovation, and storytelling methods used by prominent Urdu fiction writers.

1857 Ki Jang aur Sadiq-ul-Akhbar, Delhi

An important historical research paper examining the role of Urdu journalism during the 1857 War of Independence. The study evaluates how Urdu newspapers contributed to anti-colonial awareness and resistance narratives during British rule.

Akhbar “Sahar-e-Samiri” Lucknow

A detailed scholarly exploration of one of the important historical Urdu newspapers of Lucknow. The research highlights its literary, cultural, and social influence in Awadh during the 19th century.

Urdu Main Afsane Ki Riwayat

An academic contribution published through educational platforms such as IGNOU/eGyanKosh, focusing on the development and tradition of Urdu short stories for university-level learners and researchers.
